B — AI UX & transparency

How should we surface model/provider status and AI feature discoverability?

A

Status indicator in toolbar

A small pill/badge in the notebook toolbar showing the active provider + model (e.g. "Gemini 1.5 Flash"). Clicking it opens the Settings panel directly to the AI tab. Ghost-text and inline-chat modes shown as icons that toggle on/off.

Pros

  • Always visible, zero hunt
  • One click to change model

Cons

  • Adds toolbar clutter for non-AI users
B

Richer Settings AI tab ★ recommended

Keep toolbar minimal but make the Settings AI tab much clearer: show which mode is active (ghost-text / inline-chat / command-palette), add short descriptions of each mode, show token budget and last-used model. Add a "Test connection" button that runs a quick ping.

Pros

  • No toolbar clutter
  • Educates about all three AI entry points
  • Test connection catches misconfigured keys immediately

Cons

  • Requires opening Settings to see status
C

Both: compact toolbar pill + richer Settings

A minimal read-only pill in the toolbar (provider name + colored dot for connection status: green/yellow/red), combined with the full Settings AI tab polish from option B.

Pros

  • Best discoverability and depth

Cons

  • Most work; toolbar pill may feel noisy
